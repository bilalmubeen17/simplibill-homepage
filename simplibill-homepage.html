Intelligence, in context

AI where it earns its keep. Humans where judgment matters.

We didn't bolt on a chatbot. SimpliBill uses AI for narrow, high-value jobs — and pairs every recommendation with industry experts who've spent years inside the work.

Advisory, not autonomous.

SimpliBill's AI layer surfaces patterns your team can't see at scale: denial clusters that point to a payer policy change, underpayment trends on a specific CPT, TFL risk on claims aging toward a deadline. Then a human decides.

Every AI recommendation routes through a review workflow. Nothing auto-applies to a rule without approval. The machine proposes. Your billing team, and ours, disposes.

  • ModuleDenial clusteringGroups recurring denials by payer, CPT, and root cause
  • ModuleUnderpayment detectionCompares posted payments against expected across contracts
  • ModuleTFL risk predictionFlags claims aging toward timely filing limits
  • ModuleRule suggestionProposes new hold rules based on denial history
  • ModulePayer anomaly detectionSurfaces behavior changes across payers and plans
